# Prompt 2 — Hero Image (Website Banner)

## Tool: Midjourney

### Prompt
```
Cinematic wide-angle shot of a sleek cyberpunk corporate headquarters, 
glass and steel tower rising through neon-drenched city fog at night. 
Blue and violet holographic data streams flow around the building exterior. 
Foreground: a polished executive boardroom visible through floor-to-ceiling 
windows, professionals in dark suits reviewing holographic AI dashboards. 
Color palette: deep obsidian, electric cyan, neon violet, chrome accents. 
Rim lighting, blue-violet volumetric fog, ultra-detailed, 8K hyperrealism. 
Futuristic yet corporate. Shot on RED camera, 24mm anamorphic lens.
```

### Negative Prompt
```
--no cartoon, anime, watermark, text, logos, daylight, warm colors, 
orange tones, blurry, low resolution, dystopian decay, rust
```

### Settings
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (`--ar 16:9`)
- **Version:** `--v 6`
- **Quality:** `--q 2`
- **Stylize:** `--s 750`
- **Full flag string:** `--ar 16:9 --v 6 --q 2 --s 750`

### Design Intent
This image anchors the website homepage. It communicates: 
"We are a serious AI company at the cutting edge, but professional."
